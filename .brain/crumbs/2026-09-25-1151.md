## Key Findings
- Quest 3: 110-degree horizontal field of view, LCD, 2064 x 2208 pixels per eye, no eye tracking. Best low-friction upgrade for the existing Quest-oriented Unity application.
- Focus Vision: advertised up-to-120-degree field of view (axis unspecified), LCD, 2448 x 2448 pixels per eye, integrated 120 Hz binocular eye tracking. Its most relevant possible gain is fixation/gaze logging to separate visual confirmation, eye/head scanning, and audio-cued responses. Manufacturer accuracy of 0.5-1.1 degrees is specified only within a central 20-degree field; individual calibration and headset validation remain required. It is not a clinical perimeter or evidence of visual-field recovery.
- Focus Vision requires a Vive OpenXR/controller port and fresh device-specific display/contrast baseline; existing LogCS values cannot be compared directly across headsets. Both headsets use LCD, so neither automatically fixes the contrast issue. HTC's 120-degree maximum is not directly comparable with Meta's explicitly horizontal 110 degrees.
