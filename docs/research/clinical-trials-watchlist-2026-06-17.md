# Clinical Trials Watchlist Refresh - 2026-06-17

This is the June 17 refresh of the NegletFix registry-backed watchlist. It is a research operations artifact, not medical advice.

## Bottom Line

- The original nine tracked NCT rows were rechecked on ClinicalTrials.gov; no status changed from the June 11 baseline.
- Eight new-to-monitor NCT rows were promoted into the watchlist, mostly compensation/function/device lanes plus one exact post-stroke audiovisual stimulation trial that is not yet recruiting.
- No new registry result justifies changing the active NegletFix protocol. Open-loop, field-map-guided Quest audiovisual training remains the core route; stimulation and device adjuncts remain clinician/research questions or compensation comparators.
