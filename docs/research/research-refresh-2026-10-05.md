## Decision

No new result justifies changing the current Quest audiovisual protocol. The next product work remains the measured reprise after the Quest returns: mix left `-5°` and harder `-8°` targets, add catch/probe trials, bound the contrast staircase, and keep field mapping separate from rehabilitation. ## PubMed and MRI

PubMed E-utilities entry-date search for September 26–October 5 returned five broad hemianopia/visual-field-loss hits; one is a relevant mechanism paper, not a rehabilitation trial. `PM-013`: [Roelofzen et al., *Brain Structure & Function*, PMID 42799918](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42799918/), published September 26, used 7 T MRI to examine the stria of Gennari after loss of visual input from lesions **between the optic chiasm and V1**. The authors found preserved integrity in deprived V1. This does not show that damaged right occipital tissue can regenerate, predict Eric's outcome, or validate any Quest treatment. The remaining hits were glaucoma/diagnostic or otherwise unrelated to rehabilitation.

## Vielight and other channels

The Instagram claim was checked against the [manufacturer research library](https://www.vielight.com/research/) and primary papers. A [sham-controlled EEG pilot](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31004126/) supports the narrower claim that transcranial/intranasal near-infrared photobiomodulation can alter resting EEG rhythms; it does not show visual-field recovery. A [43-person post-Covid cognitive pilot](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41768981/) published in January 2026 missed its overall primary cognitive-composite endpoint (p=0.088); its visual-attention task is not perimetry, and all authors reported Vielight compensation. The Vielight Vagus [single-case EEG observation](https://www.vielight.com/research/) is hypothesis-generating, not a controlled clinical result. This is a review of older evidence surfaced by the post, **not a new October publication**. Existing queue row `YT-014` already tracks the Naeser/Vielight interview; no new intervention row or protocol parameter is promoted.

Targeted AP-HP, Mayo Clinic, INSERM, YouTube, and social-web searches did not surface a traceable, directly applicable post-September-25 result beyond the sources above. This is a bounded 10-day refresh, not a claim that every post or paper worldwide was exhaustively searched.
