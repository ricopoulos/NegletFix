July 9 highlights

What actually changed?

These are the meaningful updates, not the noisy search exhaust.

CTG-012 / NCT06136169

Registry results posted/updated 2026-07-09. Early large blind-side head scans improved 18% -> 84%; blind-side hazard detection improved 56% -> 86%; response time improved 2.7s -> 1.9s. Interpretation: strong functional compensation evidence, not restoration proof.

PM-006 / PMID42140549

The linked Archives PM&R paper gives the scanning-training result a citable PubMed anchor and maps it to objective head-scan and hazard-detection metrics.

PM-007 / CTG-016

The multi-periscopic vs Fresnel peripheral prism trial now has a randomized crossover publication. This strengthens the accommodation lane, not the restorative training lane.

New registry expansion

Ten registry rows were added, including Wake Forest multisensory rehab after cortical damage, BRIGHT home-based imaging-guided training, focused ultrasound plus immersive VR, neurofeedback-based VRT, AV telerehab, tRNS plus perceptual learning, and saccadic/biofeedback training.
