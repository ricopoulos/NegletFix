# Clinical Trials Watchlist Refresh - 2026-07-09

This is the July 9 refresh of the NegletFix registry-backed watchlist. It is a research operations artifact, not medical advice.

## Bottom Line

- The meaningful same-day registry update is `CTG-012` / [NCT06136169](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06136169): ClinicalTrials.gov posted/updated results on 2026-07-09, and the linked publication is [PMID 42140549](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42140549/).
- `CTG-012` is strong functional scanning evidence: early large head scans rose from 18% to 84%, blind-side hazard detection rose from 56% to 86%, and response time improved from 2.7s to 1.9s at 1-week post-training. It remains compensation/function evidence, not restorative AV evidence or driving clearance.
- `CTG-016` / [NCT04827147](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04827147) now has a linked randomized crossover prism paper, [PMID 42274646](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42274646/), reinforcing the accommodation lane.
- Ten new-to-monitor registry rows were added: `CTG-018` through `CTG-027`.
- No new registry or PubMed source changes the active NegletFix protocol. Open-loop, field-map-guided Quest audiovisual training remains the core route.
