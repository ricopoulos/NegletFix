August 13 highlights

What actually changed?

Signal separated from search noise.

CTG-028 / NCT07752563

New August 7 registry row: Glasgow Caledonian pilot RCT of NeuroEyeCoach/VISIOcoach plus usual care versus usual care for stroke-related homonymous visual field loss. This is scanning/function compensation, not visual restoration.

PM-010 / PMID42571232

DRIVE-study protocol paper entered PubMed on August 9 and maps to NCT07147660. It strengthens the driving/function measurement lane with an eight-week home rehab RCT and VR functional-vision test.

Paris/French lanes

AP-HP Salpetriere tACS, Fondation Rothschild blindsight/fMRI, INSERM Unity HMD audiovisual telerehab, and LMC2 IRON are worth watching now that Eric is back in Paris.

Watch-only tech updates

Neuro-JEPA moved to arXiv v3; ruv-neural is active with August commits. Both remain analytics/tooling leads, not treatment evidence.
